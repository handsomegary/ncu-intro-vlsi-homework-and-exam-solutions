Complete answers to all four original theory questions are in standalone `solution.tex` and rendered `solution.pdf`. `python verify_design.py` is a reproducible parameterized calculator; `verification.json` records declared inputs and analytical results.

The report distinguishes Elmore time constants from approximate50% delays, omits unspecified NAND internal capacitance, and states the matched-threshold assumption needed for mobility ratio 2 and balanced-inverter assumption needed for Cx=8/3 Cg. General expressions are also provided when those assumptions are absent. The supplied scan was read first and compared explicitly; see `../SOURCES.md`.
