The short-circuit equation is transcribed literally as `(VDD−Vt)^3`. Its overlap condition requires `VDD > Vtn + |Vtp|`: the polynomial is consistent if Vt denotes that sum; a factor of two is needed if Vt denotes each equal individual threshold. The source does not define that convention, so both meanings are explained without silently changing the printed equation.
