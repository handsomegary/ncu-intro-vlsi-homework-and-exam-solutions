# Sources and corrections

The three Fall 2012 question pages form one complete final. Duplicated or partial copies are not additional papers. Printed footers, equations and circuit contacts govern the reconstruction.

Two distinct student answer sets, with eight distinct reference pages, were read in full after independent derivation. Grades do not validate answers. `student-comparison.csv` and per-question solution paragraphs state what was retained, corrected or rejected.

The [official NCU ch06 lecture](https://www.ee.ncu.edu.tw/jfli/public_html/VLSI/lecture/ch06.pdf) is a primary conceptual reference for dynamic, domino, NP and fully differential logic. Original source connectivity and parameters govern all figures and calculations.

The Q7 net tables were cross-checked against corresponding original layouts in the2013/2016 originals; the2012 physical contacts were inspected before reuse. Q7(b) differs from the2016 counterpart and was not copied from that topology. Named-net schematics retain12MOS per layout.

No source text is missing and no prompt was inferred from student answers. Q4(b)'s inconsistent numerical constraints and Q6's unsigned skew/unprovided wire delay are explicitly answered conditionally or by infeasibility rather than silently changed.
