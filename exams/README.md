## Available papers and scope

The table lists 20 reconstructed papers from 2007 through 2024. The coverage catalog identifies complete questions, partial papers and missing premises.

Material without an identifiable question paper is omitted. This includes student-only material attributed to 2020, 2019 midterm, 2018 midterm, 2017, 2016 midterm and 2015 midterm. Duplicated 2012/2013 answer pages do not establish a separate 2013 midterm. Missing questions are not reconstructed from student answers.

Printed footers and circuit connections govern year and term attribution. The 2016 final follows its printed Fall 2016 date. Spring 2008 midterm and Fall 2008 final retain their printed terms; duplicated pages and mixed-year compilations are not counted as additional papers.
